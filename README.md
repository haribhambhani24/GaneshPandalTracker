# 🕉️ Ganesh Pandal Tracker | Mumbai Ganesh Chaturthi Guide

An intuitive, mobile-first web and Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to guide devotees, tourists, and travellers visiting Mumbai during **Ganesh Chaturthi**.

🌐 **Live Website**: [mumbaiganeshpandals.com](https://mumbaiganeshpandals.com)

---

## 🌟 Overview & Key Features

- **100% In-App Navigation**: Seamless walking, driving, and cycling directions directly on the site's Leaflet map powered by OSRM routing, featuring step-by-step turn maneuvers and live GPS tracking.
- **Interactive Map & Status Rings**: Visual exploration of Mumbai's most iconic and community pandals with live crowd levels, eco-friendly badges, and 24-hour darshan indicators.
- **Curated Walking Trails (Circuits)**: Pre-planned walking itineraries (Lalbaug Trail, Girgaon Heritage Trail, Vedic Annadanam Tour) with transit connections and walking times.
- **Real-Time Aarti & Darshan Timings**: Instant countdown to next morning and evening aartis, live open/closed status, and local railway station guidance.
- **Trilingual Accessibility**: Full, instant localization in **English**, **हिंदी (Hindi)**, and **मराठी (Marathi)**.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Installable on iOS and Android devices with offline caching and responsive bottom sheets.
- **Dual Theme Support**: Beautiful dark mode basemap and high-contrast light mode with brand orange accents.
